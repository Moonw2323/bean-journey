Farm

Coffee grows on shaded hillsides at 1,500 to 2,000 metres. The cool air slows ripening and builds flavour.

Harvest

Pickers choose only the ripe red cherries by hand, returning to the same trees several times a season.

Washing and drying

The fruit is removed from the seed, then the beans dry on raised beds for about two weeks.

Roasting

We roast small batches in our roastery, watching colour and sound to bring out sweetness without burning.

Packing

Beans rest for a few days, then go into resealable bags with the roast date printed on the front.

Your cup

Grind just before brewing and enjoy. The journey ends at your table.
